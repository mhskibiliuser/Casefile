Case 001

The Missing Trophy

Trophy Hall, Ashgrove AcademyFriday, 21:40Status: Open
Founders Cup — Trophy Hall
Ashgrove Academy
21:40 — Homecoming Eve
Three Questions Remain
01 — The Setup

A trophy vanishes on the eve of Homecoming.

The Founders Cup, Ashgrove Academy's oldest honor, was locked in its display case ahead of the Homecoming dance. By morning, the case's glass lay shattered inward — struck from within — and the cup was gone.

02 — The Location

Trophy Hall, Ashgrove Academy.

A narrow hall connecting the gymnasium to the east stairwell, with a storage room and single window along one side. Dozens of students passed through it during the dance.

03 — The Time

Friday, 21:40.

The last confirmed sighting of the trophy — caught by chance in a bystander's photo, even as the security cameras sat dark for twelve unexplained minutes.
